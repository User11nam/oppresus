

Nullus Sol: An ancient field where oil used to be mined, hot and humid, often nobody goes here population is around 300, all insane evil people who needed to hide from horrid punishment

Corton Sea: A semi-popular inhabited area, the water near the Nullus sol coast is unsafe to be in, but the land on the other side is full of life and prosperity, there is a nice community here, kinda like a humble fishing village. Population is about 360,000. The trade hub is also here, so all imports and exports come through here.

Veskan sea: Large mountains islands, most of the naval based military is trained here, so 100’s of military camps are set up, most normal people are unallowed to go other than on tours, there is one town however on the other side of the large mountain island on a little bit of flat land. Population is roughly 2,600,000

Mount Veston: Nobody is permitted to go here. Cilla’s cave is located here, it is guarded as the society’s best kept secret as it is where Cilla inhabited the first emperor, the entire mountain is off limits to all. Population is about 5 and they are all lifelong guards.

Incarcer: Land of prison, same style as swedish prisons just on a larger scale, many small spread out homes. Essentially a town of rehabilitating prisoners. One large prison called Incarculuss is built close to the edge of Nullus sol (where there is a slight bend in the red line on the map) it is used as a torture prison, where people are put under white room torture for only the most heinous acts, kinda like a replacement to the death penalty) Population: 15000

Solum: also known as Mount Solum, it is the loneliest mountain, with a population of 30 on the entire mountain, mostly in one small town around the base, but there is a wetlands area with roughly 50000 people near the base, leading into the capital. The road of trade is also here, so imports and exports from the Corton Sea pass through here into the capital. The towns are spread out across this road

Capital: The capital is a bustling city with roughly 14 million people; almost all the merchants live here. The emperor has a house here, and 6 of the 8 advisors are always here; the other two are usually split between the Veskan sea and incarcer. Almost the entire place looks like a mix of New York and Beijing, with tons of people everywhere, streets, and tons of money being made. The only non-bustling part of the city is the coast of Cura Aliud; that place is kinda a ghost town, with broken-down houses, fallen skyscrapers, and nobody in sight.

Planities: this is the flatlands and the farming area; almost all of the agricultural resources are farmed here, the land here is strangely fertile, and nobody knows why; some say it has something to do with Cura Aliud. Population, 4.3 million

Malum Rec: The darkest are, trapped between two mountains the sun sun only shines her for part of the day, massive underground weapons trade and anything that people dont want others knowing about happens here. The population is unknown but estimated between 2-5 million. People on the run stay here, people in trouble stay here, people who need protection stay here, and the government doesnt stick their nose here in fear of a revolt. Traders often pass here between the mining city and the capital. 

Sovalus: the mining city, this entire city has a fuel of mining. The merchants buy from them and sell them food from the capital and Planities, kinda forming a trade route. They have a population of around 1,440,000

Apricus: called apricus due to the large amounts of sunlight, Also a giant forest. People are not allowed to harm the wildlife here so the forest kept growing, Theres still many many towns and houses, they are just built around the vast forrest. Population 2 million.

Cura Aliad: The “dark” sea, stories are told of people coming here and never coming back, trade never comes through this side, people dont venture on this side, and the whole thing is treated as secluded as Mount Veston. Places along the coast are often abandoned. People tell stories of voices coming from the sea.
